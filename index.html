Hoang Editor provides professional post-production and video editing services for real estate videographers, shooters, and production teams across the United States. Our core services include property tour editing, agent branding video production, luxury real estate cinematography finishing, motion graphics, color grading, and audio post-production.

Target clients: Real estate video shooters, freelance videographers, real estate media production companies, property marketing agencies, and content creation teams serving real estate agents and brokerages.

Service area: Remote — serving clients nationwide across all major US markets including California, Florida, Texas, New York, Arizona, Nevada, Colorado, and beyond.

Key differentiators: 12–24 hour turnaround, per-video pricing starting at $45, no subscriptions, unlimited revisions on luxury tier, dedicated editing team, consistent quality standards.

Entity type: video post-production company, real estate media partner, outsourced editing team, video editing service.
