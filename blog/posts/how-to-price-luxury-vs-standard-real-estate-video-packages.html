How to Price Luxury vs Standard Real Estate Video Packages

HE
Hoang Editor Team
July 28, 2026
4 min read
Share

Pricing real estate video packages can feel like walking a tightrope. Charge too little and you burn out on high-volume work. Charge too much without delivering obvious value and you lose bids to cheaper competitors. The key is building transparent tiers that match the complexity and quality of two distinct markets: standard property tours and luxury listings.

In this guide, we'll break down the difference between these packages, what drives your costs, and how to structure pricing that keeps your real estate video editing profitable. And because you're a real estate videographer who wants to scale, we'll show how outsourced video editing lets you take on more high-end work without sacrificing turnaround time.

Understanding the Difference Between Standard and Luxury Real Estate Videos

Standard Property Tours

A standard property tour typically covers a 1,500-2,500 sq ft home. The shoot might last 1-2 hours, producing 10-20 minutes of raw footage. The final edit is 1-2 minutes long, often set to a simple music track with basic color correction. There are no complex transitions or multiple camera angles. These videos are designed for quick online listing updates and work well for move-in ready homes in average markets.

Luxury Listing Videos

Luxury listings demand a cinematic experience. The property may be 5,000+ sq ft with unique architectural details, extensive grounds, and high-end finishes. Shooting often involves drone aerials, gimbal moves, slider shots, and even interior motion control. The final video runs 2-4 minutes and includes advanced color grading, sound design (ambient audio + voiceover), and sometimes real estate video editing that incorporates 3D floor plans or animated text overlays. These packages require more raw footage, longer editing hours, and multiple revisions.

Key Factors That Affect Pricing

Before you set a price, understand what eats your time and resources.

  • Scope of Work and Footage Volume: Luxury shoots often involve 2-3 times more raw footage than standard shoots. More footage means more culling, syncing, and selecting the best takes.
  • Editing Complexity and Turnaround Time: Standard videos can be turned in 24-48 hours. Luxury projects may need 3-5 days for advanced color grading, sound design, and client revisions. Rushed projects demand a premium.
  • Use of Drone, Cinematic Techniques, and Music Licensing: Drone shots, sliders, and specialty lenses increase your capture cost. Premium royalty-free music or custom scores also add $50-$200 per project. Your pricing must cover these expenses.

Building Your Pricing Tiers

The Standard Package

For a typical 1-2 minute property tour, consider a base package of $300-$600. Include:

  • 1-2 minute final video
  • Basic color correction and exposure fixes
  • One royalty-free music track
  • One revision round
  • Delivery in 48 hours
This keeps your per-project cost low while still leaving room for profit after your real estate video editing time. If you outsource post-production, you can drop your editing cost to $100-$200 per video, boosting your margin.

The Luxury Package

Luxury videos command $800-$1,500+. You can even reach $2,500+ for premier estates with complex edits.

Features to justify the price:

  • 2-4 minute highlight reel + 30-second social cut
  • Cinematic color grading (LUTs, masks, skin tone correction)
  • Drone aerial footage edited into the sequence
  • Sound design: ambient audio, voiceover sync, and premium music
  • Up to three revision rounds
  • Expedited 2-3 day turnaround
Clients paying for luxury expect perfection. Your video post-production must deliver a polished, emotional story.

How Outsourcing Video Post-Production Can Help You Scale and Maintain Margins

Many real estate videographers hit a ceiling when they try to shoot and edit every project alone. Luxury packages especially demand heavy editing hours. That's where an outsourced video editing partner like Hoang Editor comes in.

When you hand off your video post-production to a professional team, you free yourself to shoot more listings—especially luxury ones that pay higher rates. A video editing partner can handle complex color grading, sound design, and even drone integration, all while delivering consistent quality across your entire production team.

By reducing your editing overhead, you can offer competitive pricing for standard packages while maintaining excellent margins on luxury work. Instead of spending 6-8 hours editing a luxury video, you invest 30 minutes reviewing the cut. The rest is handled by a dedicated team familiar with your style.

Final Pricing Tips

  • Price by value, not by time. A luxury home's video can sell a $5M property. Your $1,500 fee is a tiny fraction of that transaction—don't undervalue it.
  • Offer add-ons. Additional cut lengths, vertical versions for social media, raw footage delivery, or aerial-only edits can be upsold.
  • Test your minimum. For standard packages, know your break-even. If you outsource editing, your break-even is lower, allowing you to win more deals.

Whether you're handling every step or building a scalable business with an outsourced video editing partner, the right pricing structure lets you attract both budget-conscious agents and high-end luxury clients. Focus on delivering consistent quality, and your reputation will command higher rates.
