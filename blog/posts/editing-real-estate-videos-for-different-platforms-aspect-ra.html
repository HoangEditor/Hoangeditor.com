Editing Real Estate Videos for Different Platforms: Aspect Ratios and Durations

Why Platform-Specific Editing Matters

As a real estate videographer, you know that a stunning property tour can fall flat if it's not optimized for the platform where it's published. Different social media channels have different aspect ratio requirements and ideal video durations. Understanding these nuances is key to maximizing engagement and impressing clients. Whether you handle editing in-house or rely on outsourced video editing, tailoring each cut is essential.

Understanding Aspect Ratios

Aspect ratio refers to the width-to-height proportion of your video. Here are the most common ones for real estate content:

  • 16:9 ( Landscape ) – Standard for YouTube, websites, and presentations. Ideal for property tour videos that need to showcase wide interiors and exteriors.
  • 1:1 ( Square ) – Works well on Facebook and Instagram feeds. It provides a balanced view but may crop important details if not planned.
  • 9:16 ( Vertical ) – Designed for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Perfect for quick, attention-grabbing walkthroughs.
  • 4:5 ( Portrait ) – A slightly taller ratio popular on Facebook and Instagram feeds, offering more vertical space than 1:1 without being full vertical.
Always shoot with multiple aspect ratios in mind, or leave enough safe area in your video post-production to crop later.

Optimal Durations by Platform

The length of your video can make or break viewer retention. Here’s a quick guide:

  • YouTube – 2 to 5 minutes for full property tours, 30-60 seconds for teasers or community highlights.
  • Instagram Feed – 30 to 60 seconds. Short, engaging snippets work best.
  • Instagram Reels / TikTok – 15 to 30 seconds. Focus on the most striking features with fast pacing.
  • Facebook – 1 to 2 minutes. Slightly longer than Instagram, but still concise.
  • LinkedIn – 30 to 90 seconds for professional networking and commercial properties.

Your video editing partner should understand these preferences and deliver platform-ready files.

Editing Strategies for Each Platform

YouTube & Website

For YouTube, use 16:9 with a cinematic property tour feel. Include smooth transitions, drone shots, and detailed room coverage. Add captions and lower-thirds for agent branding. Duration can extend to 5 minutes if the property is large or the editing is dynamic.

Instagram & Facebook Feeds

Square (1:1) or vertical (4:5) formats work well here. Keep videos under 60 seconds. Start with an attention-grabbing hook—like a stunning exterior shot or a sleek kitchen reveal. Use text overlays to highlight key selling points since many users watch without sound.

TikTok & Reels

Vertical 9:16 is non-negotiable. Aim for 15 to 30 seconds with fast cuts, trending audio, and quick text callouts. Show only the most impressive rooms or features. A production team familiar with short-form trends can help you stay relevant.

LinkedIn

For commercial real estate or networking, 16:9 or 1:1 works. Keep it professional and informative. A 60-second highlight reel with voiceover or text can be very effective.

Best Practices for Multi-Platform Delivery

  • Plan ahead: Shoot extra vertical footage or use a camera that captures high resolution for cropping.
  • Use templates: Create reusable intro/outro animations for different ratios.
  • Batch export: After editing the master 16:9 version, create crop versions for 1:1, 4:5, and 9:16.
  • Test and analyze: Monitor engagement metrics and adjust durations accordingly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need different aspect ratios for real estate videos?

Different social platforms favor specific aspect ratios, such as 16:9 for YouTube and 9:16 for TikTok. A real estate video editing service ensures your property tour looks great everywhere.

How long should a real estate video be for Instagram?

For Instagram feed posts, 30 to 60 seconds works best, while Reels should be 15 to 30 seconds. A video editing partner can trim your footage to the ideal duration.

Can I use the same video file for all platforms?

Technically yes, but it may get cropped awkwardly or lose impact. Outsourced video editing allows you to receive platform-specific versions with proper framing and pacing.

What is the best aspect ratio for a property tour video?

For a full property tour, 16:9 landscape is standard for YouTube and websites. However, a real estate videographer can also deliver vertical cuts for social media teasers.
