Horizontal vs Vertical: Video Deliverables for Modern Agents

Introduction to Video Deliverables

In today's digital age, real estate video editing and post-production play a crucial role in showcasing properties to potential buyers. As a real estate videographer or production team, it's essential to understand how to package video deliverables that meet the needs of modern agents and their online platforms.

Horizontal vs Vertical Video

With the rise of social media, vertical video has become increasingly popular, particularly on platforms like Instagram and TikTok. However, horizontal video remains the standard for property tours and website embeds. As an outsourced video editing partner, it's vital to offer both formats to cater to different agent needs.

Horizontal Video

Horizontal video is ideal for property tours, as it provides a more immersive and cinematic experience. This format is also better suited for website embeds, allowing agents to showcase properties in a more traditional and engaging way.

Vertical Video

Vertical video, on the other hand, is perfect for social media platforms, where users are more likely to consume content on their mobile devices. By offering vertical video deliverables, agents can share property previews, teasers, or snippets on social media, driving engagement and interest in the property.

Packaging Video Deliverables

When packaging video deliverables, consider the following formats and resolutions to ensure compatibility with various platforms:

  • Horizontal video: 1080p or 4K, 16:9 aspect ratio
  • Vertical video: 1080p or 4K, 9:16 aspect ratio
  • Mobile-friendly formats: MP4, MOV, or AVI

As a video editing partner, it's essential to communicate with agents to understand their specific needs and preferences. By offering customized video deliverables, you can help agents showcase properties effectively and efficiently.

Conclusion

In conclusion, understanding the difference between horizontal and vertical video is crucial for real estate videographers and production teams. By offering both formats and packaging video deliverables correctly, you can help modern agents succeed in their online marketing efforts. Remember, as an outsourced video editing partner, your goal is to provide high-quality video post-production services that meet the unique needs of each agent.

By providing both horizontal and vertical video formats, you can help agents reach a wider audience and increase engagement on social media and property websites.
