Vertical vs Horizontal Real Estate Videos: What Agents Actually Need in 2025

October 19, 2026
4 min read
Share

The Shift to Vertical Video in Real Estate Marketing

Vertical video is now a must for social platforms. Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts have changed how buyers discover homes. Agents need short, engaging clips that capture attention within seconds. For a real estate videographer, this means capturing footage that can be cropped or re-framed for 9:16 without losing key details.

Vertical formats require fast-paced editing, bold text overlays, and smooth transitions. Video post-production plays a huge role in turning raw footage into scroll-stopping content. Many videographers rely on an outsourced video editing partner to handle the platform-specific cutdowns.

Why Horizontal Video Still Dominates MLS and Websites

Despite the vertical trend, horizontal 16:9 video remains the standard for MLS listings, YouTube full tours, and agent websites. A cinematic property tour in horizontal format allows buyers to see the flow of a home, often with narration or ambient sound. Agents embed these videos on listing pages and share them via email.

Horizontal video editing focuses on color grading, stabilization, and smooth camera movement. A professional video editing partner can deliver both cinematic horizontal cuts and dynamic vertical versions from the same footage.

What Agents Actually Need in 2025: A Hybrid Approach

Agents don't want to choose between formats—they need both. The best strategy is to shoot once with a hybrid mindset. That means recording in 4K or higher, leaving safe areas for cropping, and planning shots that work in both aspect ratios.

Here's what a typical agent might request:

  • A full property tour in horizontal 16:9 for MLS and YouTube.
  • 3–5 vertical short clips (15–30 seconds) for Instagram Reels and TikTok.
  • Consistent branding, captions, and music across all versions.

To deliver this efficiently, many videographers build a relationship with a production team that specializes in real estate editing. This allows them to focus on shooting while the team handles real estate video editing for multiple formats.

How Outsourced Video Editing Simplifies the Workflow

Managing two formats in-house can be time-consuming. An outsourced video editing service can take your raw footage and deliver both vertical and horizontal cuts, complete with motion graphics, color correction, and captions.

We used to spend hours re-editing the same footage for different platforms. Now our editing partner delivers vertical cutdowns the same day.

By partnering with a dedicated video editing partner, you scale your output without hiring more staff. Your production team can handle multiple client projects simultaneously.

Technical Tips for Shooting Both Formats

To get the most out of your footage for both vertical and horizontal delivery, follow these practices:

  • Shoot in 4K (or higher) so you can crop into vertical without losing resolution.
  • Use a gimbal for smooth movement that works in any aspect ratio.
  • Keep important subjects centered or within a safe zone for 9:16 crop.
  • Capture extra vertical b-roll using a smartphone or rotate the camera.
  • Record separate audio if needed, as vertical cutdowns may not use the original narration.

These techniques ensure that your real estate video editing process is efficient and the final products look professional.

Conclusion

In 2025, real estate agents need video content that performs on every platform. Vertical videos drive engagement on social media, while horizontal videos remain essential for listings and long-form tours. As a real estate videographer, offering both formats—and handling them through a reliable video editing partner—will set you apart. Consider outsourcing your video post-production to scale your business and deliver what agents actually need.
