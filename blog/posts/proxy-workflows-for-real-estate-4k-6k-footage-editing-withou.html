Introduction to Proxy Workflows

As a real estate videographer, you understand the importance of capturing high-quality footage to showcase properties in the best possible light. With the increasing demand for 4K and 6K resolution videos, editing large files can be a daunting task, especially when working with limited computer resources. This is where proxy workflows come into play, allowing you to edit your footage without experiencing timeline lag.

What are Proxy Workflows?

A proxy workflow involves creating lower-resolution versions of your original footage, called proxies, which are used for editing purposes. These proxies are then replaced with the original high-resolution files during the final export, ensuring that your video retains its original quality.

Benefits of Proxy Workflows

  • Reduced timeline lag and improved editing performance
  • Ability to work with high-resolution footage on lower-end computers
  • Faster rendering and export times

Implementing Proxy Workflows for Real Estate Video Editing

To implement a proxy workflow for your real estate video editing projects, you'll need to follow these steps:

  1. Import your 4K/6K footage into your video editing software
  2. Create proxies for each clip, using a lower resolution such as 1080p or 2K
  3. Edit your project using the proxy files
  4. Replace the proxies with the original high-resolution files during the final export
