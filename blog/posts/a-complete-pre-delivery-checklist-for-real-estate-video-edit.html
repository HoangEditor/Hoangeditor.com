Why a Pre-Delivery Checklist Matters

In real estate video editing, the final delivery is your handshake with the client. A polished property tour can win repeat business, but a missed detail can cost you trust. Whether you're a solo real estate videographer or a production team, a systematic pre-delivery checklist ensures every video leaves your studio flawless. This checklist is also the backbone of a reliable video editing partner—it turns quality from a hope into a habit.

1. Technical Quality Checks

Before uploading, scrutinize the fundamentals of video post-production. These are the non-negotiables that separate professional work from amateur edits.

Resolution and Frame Rate

  • Confirm the export matches the source footage and the client's delivery spec (e.g., 4K at 24fps or 30fps).
  • Check for accidental cropping, scaling artifacts, or letterboxing.
  • Ensure bitrate is sufficient to avoid compression artifacts, especially in high-motion areas like drone fly-throughs.

Color and Exposure

  • Verify that color grading is consistent across all shots, especially transitional spaces like hallways and stairwells.
  • Monitor for overexposed windows or underexposed interiors; dynamic range should feel natural.
  • Check skin tones if people appear (e.g., agents or homeowners).

Audio and Music

  • Ensure background music is licensed and mixed at an appropriate level (usually -12 to -18 dB for dialogue-free videos).
  • Check for sync issues with any voiceover or ambient sounds (e.g., door chimes, footsteps).
  • Fade audio in/out smoothly at the beginning and end.

Playback and Device Testing

Watch the exported file on at least two devices (computer and phone) to catch issues like stutter, audio drift, or color shifts that a monitor might hide.

"A technically flawless video speaks louder than any marketing promise. Your video editing partner should sweat these details so you don't have to."

2. Content Accuracy

The property tour must reflect reality and the client's requested narrative. A single mislabeled room or outdated price can derail a sale.

  • Cross-check the property address, price, and agent contact info against the brief.
  • Verify that the shot order follows the natural flow of the home (exterior → entry → living spaces → bedrooms → outdoor).
  • Ensure all text overlays, lower thirds, and captions are spell-checked and properly timed.
  • Confirm that any featured amenities (pool, gym, smart home features) are accurately represented—no mismatched footage from another listing.

3. Client-Specific Requirements

Every agent or brokerage has preferences. Did you honor them?

  • Brand elements: logo placement, watermark, intro/outro animation.
  • Video length: typically 1–3 minutes for a standard listing, but confirm any special cutdowns for social media.
  • Music genre and tempo: upbeat for modern, classical for luxury, etc.
  • Any required disclaimers or fair housing statements.
  • Specific platform dimensions: vertical for Instagram Reels/TikTok, square for Facebook, or 16:9 for YouTube/MLS.

4. Final Export and Delivery

Even the best edit can fail if the file is corrupt or misnamed.

  • Export using a high-quality codec (H.264 or ProRes) at the correct aspect ratio.
  • Name files clearly: e.g., "123_Main_St_4K_Final_v2.mp4".
  • Upload to the agreed platform (Dropbox, Vimeo, Frame.io) and send a delivery notification with a summary of what was delivered.
  • Double-check that the file opens without errors and that the audio/video tracks are intact.

For many real estate videographers, managing these checks on every job is time-consuming. That's why outsourced video editing is growing: a dedicated video editing partner can implement this checklist religiously, leaving you to focus on shooting and client relationships. When your production team trusts the process, every delivery becomes a testament to your professionalism.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does real estate video editing typically take?

For a standard 2-3 minute property tour, most outsourced video editing partners deliver within 24-48 hours, depending on complexity.

What file formats do you accept for real estate footage?

We accept common formats like MP4, MOV, and ProRes, and can work with footage from any camera or drone used by real estate videographers.

Can you edit vertical videos for social media?

Yes, our video post-production team can repurpose a property tour into vertical, square, or other aspect ratios for platforms like Instagram and TikTok.

How do I ensure my client's branding is included?

Provide your logo files and brand guidelines to your video editing partner, and we'll apply them consistently across every delivery.
