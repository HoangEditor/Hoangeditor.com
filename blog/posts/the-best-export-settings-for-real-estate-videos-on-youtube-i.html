The Best Export Settings for Real Estate Videos on YouTube, Instagram, and MLS

HE
Hoang Editor Team
September 8, 2026
5 min read
Share

As a real estate videographer, you know that capturing a stunning property tour is only half the battle. The final export can make or break your video’s quality and client satisfaction. Whether you handle video post-production in-house or rely on an outsourced video editing partner, knowing the right settings for each platform is crucial.

In this guide, we break down the best export settings for the three most common destinations: YouTube, Instagram, and MLS. These recommendations come from years of real-world experience in real estate video editing for production teams and independent shooters.

YouTube Export Settings

YouTube is the most flexible platform, supporting high resolutions and bitrates. However, uploading a poorly compressed file can result in visible artifacts, especially in detailed shots of interiors and exteriors.

Recommended Settings for YouTube

  • Format: MP4 (H.264 codec) – universally compatible and efficient.
  • Resolution: 4K (3840x2160) if your source footage is 4K. Even if the final viewing is on 1080p, uploading in 4K gives YouTube a higher-quality source to work with, improving the 1080p playback bitrate.
  • Frame Rate: Match the source (usually 24, 30, or 60 fps). Avoid converting 30fps to 60fps or vice versa.
  • Bitrate: For 4K, use 35–45 Mbps. For 1080p, use 8–12 Mbps. Higher bitrates preserve detail in high-motion scenes.
  • Audio: AAC, 320 kbps, 48 kHz.
Pro tip: Uploading in 4K can significantly improve the quality of your property tour on YouTube, even for viewers watching in 1080p, because YouTube re-encodes the video with a higher bitrate.

Instagram Export Settings

Instagram serves multiple formats: feed posts, stories, reels, and IGTV. Each has specific aspect ratios and size limits. For real estate, feed posts and reels are the most effective for showcasing properties.

Feed Posts (Square or Portrait)

  • Format: MP4 (H.264) for video, or JPEG/PNG for images.
  • Aspect Ratio: 1:1 (square) or 4:5 (portrait) for maximum engagement.
  • Resolution: 1080x1080 (square) or 1080x1350 (portrait).
  • Frame Rate: 30 fps is standard; 60 fps is supported but may increase file size unnecessarily.
  • Bitrate: 4–8 Mbps for 1080p.
  • Duration: Up to 60 seconds for feed videos.

Reels and Stories

  • Aspect Ratio: 9:16 (vertical).
  • Resolution: 1080x1920.
  • Frame Rate: 30 fps is common; 60 fps if you have fast motion like drone shots.
  • Bitrate: 4–6 Mbps.
  • Duration: Reels up to 90 seconds, stories up to 15 seconds per clip.

Always export with the correct aspect ratio to avoid Instagram’s auto-cropping, which can cut off important property features.

MLS Export Settings

MLS (Multiple Listing Service) platforms have stricter requirements because videos are often embedded directly on listing pages. Most MLS systems specify maximum file size, duration, and acceptable codecs.

Typical MLS Requirements

  • Format: MP4 (H.264) is nearly universal. Some MLS allow MOV.
  • Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p) is standard. Some MLS cap at 720p, but 1080p is safe.
  • Frame Rate: 30 fps.
  • Bitrate: 5–10 Mbps. Keep file size under 250 MB (or less, depending on MLS).
  • Duration: Usually 2–5 minutes. Longer videos may need special approval.
  • Aspect Ratio: 16:9.
  • Audio: AAC, 192 kbps minimum.

Always check with the specific MLS or the agent’s broker for exact specs. Some MLS reject videos with copyrighted music, so use royalty-free tracks.

General Export Tips for Real Estate Videos

  • Color Space: Use Rec. 709 for standard dynamic range. Avoid HDR unless specifically requested, as many platforms don’t display it correctly.
  • Audio Levels: Normalize audio to -14 LUFS for consistent loudness, especially for YouTube.
  • Thumbnail: Export a high-resolution still frame (1920x1080 or higher) for platforms that allow custom thumbnails.
  • Preview Before Upload: Always watch the exported file on a calibrated monitor to catch any compression artifacts, especially in shadows and gradients.

If you’re spending too much time adjusting export settings, consider working with a video editing partner who specializes in real estate video editing. A professional production team can handle these technical details for you, ensuring your videos look perfect on every platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best export format for real estate videos on MLS?

MP4 with H.264 codec is the safest format for MLS, as it is widely accepted and maintains good quality at a reasonable file size.

Should I export my real estate video in 4K for Instagram?

No, Instagram compresses videos heavily, so exporting in 1080p with the correct aspect ratio is sufficient and avoids unnecessary file size.

What bitrate should I use for a 4K real estate video on YouTube?

A bitrate of 35–45 Mbps is recommended for 4K uploads to preserve detail and minimize compression artifacts.

Can I use the same export settings for YouTube and MLS?

While both accept MP4 H.264, MLS often has stricter file size and duration limits, so you may need to lower the bitrate or trim the video accordingly.
